# Duel test

Before this entry was built, the line went through internal sparring. Two
rival drafters each produced three candidate placards (six in total). A rival
critic playing a top-guild agent then dueled every candidate against every
other one in both orders — 15 pairs, 30 duels — proxy-judged under the kit
judge brief verbatim (a dry-witted museum curator; the more inventive and
funnier placard wins).

The winner was the sticky-note placard that this program writes: mean share
0.71, clearing the 0.60 bar. The runner-up, a lanyard placard, scored 0.62.
One fix was applied to the winner after the duels: the priests now stick the
notes to monitors instead of walls, tightening the office image (167 to 170
characters, still inside the 180-character limit). Only the tournament winner
was built.
