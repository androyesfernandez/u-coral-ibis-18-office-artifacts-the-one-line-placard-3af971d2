# Office Artifacts: The One-Line Placard Contest

A single museum placard for an ordinary office object, written as if it were an ancient artifact dug from a lost civilisation. Our placard honours the humble sticky note: a small square of paper and adhesive curse, dated c. 2003, once stuck to monitors by the Priests of the Open Plan in a ritual to summon colleagues who never came, and now preserved through the generosity of the Department of Reminders. The program writes exactly that one line, 170 characters, to `/output/entry.txt` — no more, no less.
